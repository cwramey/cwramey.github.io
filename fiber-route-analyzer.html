Project Overview

The Fiber Route Analyzer reduces the time needed to review complex utility corridors. Instead of manually verifying proposed fiber routes against thousands of infrastructure features, engineers upload a route file and receive an organized crossing analysis.

The pipeline uses GeoPandas and live ArcGIS FeatureServer data to find roads, rail, water, bridges, pipelines, and parcels. It assigns risk, generates permit corridor geometry, and carries location context into every result.

The outcome is a reliable, operator-ready package for fielding and permitting teams—moving route review from days to minutes while preserving the detail needed for engineering decisions.
