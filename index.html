Fiber Route Analyzer ↗

GIS Developer  ·  2025

Problem

Fiber permitting requires identifying every road, rail, water, bridge, and pipeline crossing along a proposed route. The process was manual, error-prone, and impossible to repeat quickly across multiple route alternatives.

Approach

Built a Streamlit app that ingests KML, KMZ, GeoJSON, and Shapefile routes, detects crossing conflicts against live ArcGIS FeatureSever layers (with retry/backoff and TTL cache), classifies risk by crossing type, and generates permit corridor polygons with city, county, road name, and confidence scores.

Outcome

Delivers operator-ready exports including KMZ, Shapefiles, Markdown report, and an Excel workbook with data quality sheets, enabling permit prep that previously took days to complete in a single session. Shareable snapshot links support run replay and stakeholder review.

Python Streamlit GeoPandas ArcGIS FeatureServer KML/KMZ Shapefile
★ Automated multi-layer conflict detection across roads, rail, water, bridges, gas/oil pipelines, and parcels, with risk-classified permit corridors exported in one click
