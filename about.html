// About Me

I'm a GIS and geospatial systems leader with 20+ years in telecom and infrastructure, from drafting OSP fiber routes early in my career to architecting enterprise ArcGIS platforms and managing a 15-person GIS team. Today I lead GIS for FTTH fielding operations while still writing the automation myself.

I design systems that connect GIS data to real operational outcomes: standardized enterprise schemas, CAD-to-GIS pipelines, and production tools that have cut multi-day permitting and reporting work down to minutes across thousands of miles of fiber deployment. A few of them are in my portfolio.

What I care about is the boring-but-critical part: data that validates itself, automation a team can trust in production, and solutions that make sense to both engineers and the business. My best work is where geospatial data directly drives infrastructure in the field.

What I Build

  • End-to-end GIS workflows for utility and infrastructure programs (power, gas, telecom, EV charging)
  • Automation pipelines for analysis, QA/QC, reporting, and recurring operations
  • Data integration systems bridging GIS, Excel, PDFs, and business reporting layers
  • Production-ready geospatial apps for route analysis, permitting, and decision support

How I Work

  • Stable, testable automation over one-off scripts
  • Performance and reliability: retry logic, caching, validation, error handling
  • Solutions understandable by both technical teams and business stakeholders
  • Best work where geospatial data directly supports real-world infrastructure operations
