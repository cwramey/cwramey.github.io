// About

Charles Ramey

GIS Developer & Spatial Systems Engineer · Woodstock, GA

I spent the first part of my career inside outside plant work: drafting fiber routes, preparing permits and running GIS teams for telecom and infrastructure programs. That is where I learned which steps cost a project days, and the costliest were manual ones. People checked routes against crossings one sheet at a time, re-keyed designs between CAD and GIS, and phoned field crews to learn where they were.

I now build the systems that remove those steps. My work runs from Python ETL against ArcGIS FeatureServers to full-stack applications on PostGIS and ArcGIS Enterprise. I designed and built the three flagship systems on my own, and two of them are in daily production use by design and field teams.
