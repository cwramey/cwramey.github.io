Project overview

The problem

Design plan measurements are often trapped in long, irregular PDFs, forcing manual counts and repeated checks before permit or billing work can begin.

The solution

Multiple parsing strategies identify the most reliable source on each sheet, recover the measurements, and produce clean, structured data for downstream use.

<30 secper sheet instead of manual counting

JSONready for APIs and reporting workflows

Capabilities

Recover the numbers that matter

Text-layer extraction

Reads vector PDFs with PyMuPDF, avoiding unnecessary OCR for source design sheets.

Station chains

Computes route footage from STA+offset notation and segment labels.

Footer summaries

Captures totals from standard telecom design-sheet footer tables.

Transparent results

Returns method, confidence, raw values, and a per-segment breakdown for review.
