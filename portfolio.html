Systems that telecom and utility teams run on

Each one removes a manual step from fielding, design, permitting or dispatch. The three flagship systems are designed, built and maintained by me alone.

GIS · Permitting

Fiber Route Conflict Analyzer

Checks a proposed fiber route against roads, rail, water, bridges, pipelines and parcels pulled from live FeatureServer layers, classifies each crossing by permitting risk, and writes permit corridors and operator-ready exports.

  • 1,000+ routes analyzed, about 10,000 route-miles
  • Permit prep cut from 2–3 days to under 1 hour
  • 40+ permits through submission, fewer than 1% with a missed conflict

Design · CAD/GIS

In production

Canvas: Network Design Surface

A browser-based CAD/GIS design tool that replaced a stack of separate tools. Designers import CAD, GIS and Katapult data, draw fiber networks on ArcGIS basemaps, and get the bill of materials, splice diagrams and traces generated for them.

  • Design time down 25%
  • BOM, splice diagram and trace prep down 90%; review is the only manual step
  • Up to 40 designers across 100+ work orders
